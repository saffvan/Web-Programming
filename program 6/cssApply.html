FRUITS

Fruits are nature’s sweet and nutritious gifts, packed with vitamins, minerals, and fiber that help keep our bodies healthy.
They come in a wide variety of colors, flavors, and textures—from the juicy sweetness of mangoes and oranges to the crisp freshness of apples and pears.
Eating a variety of fruits every day can boost the immune system, improve digestion, and provide natural energy.
Whether enjoyed fresh, blended into smoothies, or added to salads and desserts, fruits are an essential part of a balanced diet and a delicious way to stay healthy.
